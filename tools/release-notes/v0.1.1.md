**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.1.1

A small update: one new, experimental launcher option.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

## New

- **Compile shaders in the background (experimental)**, in the launcher's Game section. Off by
  default. New shaders compile without holding the frame, which can mean fewer pauses in areas you
  have not played yet.

  While a shader compiles, what it draws is skipped: objects can flash or be missing for a moment.
  In an older build this also crashed the game, so turn it off if you see crashes or graphics bugs.
  It only applies when you start the game from the launcher, and it makes little difference once
  your shaders are in the cache.

## Update

Extract the zip over your 0.1.0 folder, or into a new one. Your saves, settings and logs are in its
`analysis` folder: if you use a new folder, copy `analysis` across first. To keep your compiled
shaders, copy it with `analysis\tlou-user\cache` included.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**.

## Known issues

- NPCs sometimes move in jumps. It comes and goes between runs; the cause is not settled. A fixed
  30 FPS limit in the launcher had no jumps in the sessions tried.
- Short pauses the first time an area is played, while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.
- Tested on one PC, without a full playthrough. The new option had one short play session and three
  short idle runs, with no errors.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher).
