**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.1.5

The PS4 Pro's sharper shadows, and a mouse camera that keeps up with the mouse.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

## New

- **PS4 Pro shadows.** The game now uses the shadow quality of the PS4 Pro: sharper sun shadows and
  more detailed shadows from lamps and flashlights. Only the shadows change, not the rest of the
  Pro mode. It is on by default; turn it off with **PS4 Pro shadows (higher resolution)** in the
  launcher.

## Fixed

- **Slow mouse camera.** The mouse moves the camera through the right stick, and the camera never
  turned faster than the stick pushed all the way, whatever the sensitivity. It now keeps turning
  faster as you move the mouse faster.

  **The mouse is now about five times faster at the same sensitivity** (the old 500% is the new
  100%). If you had raised the sensitivity, lower it in the launcher's **Controls** or in the
  in-game menu (Insert).

## Update

Extract the zip over your previous folder, or into a new one. Your saves, settings and logs are in
its `analysis` folder: if you use a new folder, copy `analysis` across first. To keep your compiled
shaders, copy it with `analysis\tlou-user\cache` included.

## New install

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`.
4. Click **Play**.

## Known issues

- **Shots pass through some parts of objects** with no effect, though they hit walls and most
  objects where the crosshair is. One firefly pendant, hanging from a tree in the courtyard after
  the quarantine zone, cannot be shot down for that reason.
- **Intel Arc graphics cards (tested on a B580) do not work yet.** The game crashes at the start
  logos or the main menu, and the scene behind the menu is white.
- Soft shadows can look detached from objects, with a thin light outline around them and little
  darkening where they touch the ground, most visibly under vehicle wheels. The cause is not
  settled, and it may be how the original game looks. A screenshot of the same spot on PS4 would
  help.
- NPCs sometimes move in jumps. It comes and goes between runs; the cause is not settled. A fixed
  30 FPS limit in the launcher had no jumps in the sessions tried.
- Short pauses the first time an area is played, while its shaders compile.
- DLSS and FSR 4 are not in this package: DLSS falls back to FSR 3.
- A player reported the game playable from start to finish, with one crash along the way. That crash
  is not fixed yet.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher).
